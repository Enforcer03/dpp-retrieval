<unit>
TYPE: METHOD
SOURCE_CHUNK_IDS: [d3b0f4a06545913e]
TEXT: The survey categorizes feature fusion mechanisms into four main types: (1) Early fusion, combining raw or low-level modality inputs at the initial stage; (2) Intermediate fusion, merging modality-specific features during feature extraction; (3) Late fusion, integrating modality-specific decisions at the output stage; and (4) Joint fusion, hybridizing early, intermediate, and late fusion to maximize cross-modal information utilization.
</unit>

<unit>
TYPE: ARCHITECTURE

These LLMs are adapted to accept multimodal embeddings (e.g., via projection layers or cross-attention modules), enabling them to operate as the central reasoning engine over fused multimodal representations.

TEXT: Video-LLaMA’s audio branch combines audio features with positional embeddings and feeds them into an Audio Q-Former. The Audio Q-Former maps all audio segments into a fixed-length representation, which is then linearly projected to match the LLM feature dimension, enabling unified multimodal conditioning.

<unit>
TYPE: ARCHITECTURE

TEXT: Analogous to its Video Q-Former for visual data, Video-LLaMA’s Audio Q-Former provides a learnable query-based interface between continuous audio features and the LLM. This design standardizes variable-length audio into a compact set of query embeddings suitable for LLM input.
</unit>
